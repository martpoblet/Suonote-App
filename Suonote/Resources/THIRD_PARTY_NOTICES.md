# Third-Party Notices

## Arachno SoundFont

This app includes a Lite subset of Arachno SoundFont.

Arachno SoundFont is created by Maxime Abbey (Arachnosoft) and is used with permission.

Official page:
https://www.arachnosoft.com/main/soundfont.php

## Additional SoundFont packs (optional, registry-aware)

The Studio engine accepts dedicated SoundFonts per instrument family via
`SoundFontManager.swift`. None are currently bundled — the engine falls back
to Arachno Lite when a dedicated pack is absent. When new packs are added,
record their names, authors, and licenses below.

See `Suonote/SoundFonts/README.md` for the expected file paths.
